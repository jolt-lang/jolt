# Jolt, JVM Clojure and Babashka by workload category

November 10, 2026 · Seed `42` · 47 generated projects · All 7,332 executions across 564 fresh processes matched the oracle, including warmups.

**On the mixed workload, Jolt loaded in 211 ms with its namespace cache populated and executed 100 passes in 35.1 ms.** JVM Clojure took 1,152 ms to load and 17.6 ms to execute. Babashka took 77 ms to load and 35.9 ms to execute. All three produced checksum `78226`. The Jolt and Babashka execution times were close on this workload. Their per-process medians ranged from 35.0–40.5 ms and 34.6–35.9 ms, respectively.

## Startup and load

Babashka had the lowest startup/load time on every profile. Jolt loaded faster than the JVM on 2 of the 37 individual categories on the first launch (`core/control` and `dispatch/multimethods`), then on all 37 once its namespace cache was populated. The table gives milliseconds. Each column for the individual categories gives the median across the 37 rows, with equal weight per category.

## Execution differences

Across the 37 individual categories, Jolt had a lower execution median than Babashka on **28** and a lower median than JVM Clojure on **15**. Jolt had the lowest median of all three on 14, the JVM on 22 and Babashka on one (`core/text`). Nine Jolt/JVM pairs were within a factor of 1.10. These are counts for this seed and machine.

- **Filesystem paths showed the largest gap.** Across all five `babashka/fs` leaves, Jolt took 1.12–1.22 seconds per 100 passes, 11.0–15.3× the JVM time and 5.1–5.6× the Babashka time. The combined filesystem profile took 1,162 ms on Jolt, 80 ms on JVM Clojure and 219 ms on Babashka. It made 145,100 calls per sample and included path construction and hashing of the returned text.
- **Dispatch depended on the construct.** For protocols, Jolt took 53.7 ms, JVM Clojure 102.0 ms and Babashka 91.8 ms. For multimethods, JVM Clojure led at 6.9 ms, followed by Jolt at 19.4 ms and Babashka at 29.2 ms.
- **Java interop varied by class.** On byte-buffer flip, Jolt took 59.7 ms, JVM Clojure 45.4 ms and Babashka 511.7 ms. On the grouped math workload Jolt led at 31.9 ms, followed by JVM Clojure at 42.5 ms and Babashka at 100.3 ms. Duration arithmetic went the other way, with Jolt at 274.2 ms, JVM Clojure at 43.3 ms and Babashka at 212.1 ms.
- **Core constructs also differed.** Jolt took roughly half the JVM time for control flow (8.3 vs 16.7 ms). For sequences it took 199.7 vs 89.1 ms, and for state it took 212.8 vs 50.1 ms. In the macro profile, expansion contributes to startup/load. The 18.9 ms execution time on Jolt measures the generated program after expansion.

All timing columns below are milliseconds. Load is the median of three fresh launches after the first project load. Execution is the median of three process medians, each based on seven timed 100-pass executions after ten warmups.

## Measurement conditions

- **Host and versions.** Apple M3 Ultra virtual machine, 24 logical CPUs, 32 GiB RAM, macOS 15.7.3 arm64. Installed Homebrew Jolt 0.8.24, JVM Clojure 1.12.4 (CLI 1.12.4.1618, OpenJDK 25.0.2), and Babashka 1.12.218. Generator and runner at `ef51da36`.
- **Workloads.** Seed `42`, target 5,000 nonblank lines, namespace target 400 lines, runtime input `42`, 100 passes per execution. One directory per profile. All three runtimes read the same generated source for a given row. The sweep covered all 37 leaves, eight distinct multi-leaf parent selections and two controls. The mixed workload selected 20 of the 26 available Java and filesystem operations. The individual runs exercised all 26. A parent with a single leaf produces the same program as that leaf. `all` enables every category. `baseline` disables every optional category and still executes the generated workers and checksum fold.
- **Startup/load.** Wall time from process launch through the readiness marker after loading the project. Before measurement, the JVM CLI prepared each project's classpath and dependencies with `-P`, including `babashka/fs` 0.3.17 where selected. Each project's first launch used its own empty Jolt AOT cache directory. Three subsequent fresh launches reused that directory. The tables use the median of those three later launches. The OS page cache stayed enabled throughout. “First” means first launch against that project cache.
- **Execution.** Each later process ran 10 warmups and 7 timed executions. The execution columns give the median of the three process medians, in milliseconds per 100 passes. Timing covers only `workload.main/run`. Every warmup and sample restarted from the same input and was compared with the independent oracle. JVM tiered compilation and GC can continue during sampling.
- **Ordering.** The sweep ran one benchmark at a time. Within a profile, the stock runner always launched Jolt, JVM Clojure, then Babashka. Each of the four rounds used a new shuffled profile order. Startup omits dependency downloads, and execution omits launch, loading, oracle computation and I/O with the runner.
- **Reading the rows.** The size target controls source lines. It allows the amount of executed work to change across selectors. “Kernels/pass” counts generated kernel invocations from worker folds per pass. Those kernels can do very different work, including helpers, allocations, conversions and checksum arithmetic. Compare runtimes within a row. For example, the baseline invokes 3,960 small kernels per pass while the mixed workload invokes 34. The `load/unused` row includes generated orphan source outside the entry point’s require graph. The filesystem rows build paths and fold their returned text into the checksum in memory.

## Reproduce one row

From the repository root, the following reproduces the `babashka/fs` profile. Use an empty project directory. Substitute a table selector after `--only` for another row. For `all`, omit `--only`. For `baseline`, use `--exclude babashka,core,dispatch,jvm,load,syntax`.

```sh
kit="$PWD/benchmarks/generated-project"
project="$kit/out/reproduce-fs-seed42"
bb "$kit/generate.bb" "$project" --seed 42 --lines 5k --namespace-lines 400 --only babashka/fs
(
  cd "$project" || exit 1
  if rg -q ':bench-jvm' deps.edn; then
    clojure -Srepro -P -M:bench-jvm
  else
    clojure -Srepro -P -M
  fi
  export JOLT_CACHE_DIR="$PWD/aot-cache"
  bb run.bb . 42 100 --warmup 0 --samples 1
  for round in 1 2 3; do
    bb run.bb . 42 100 --warmup 10 --samples 7
  done
)
```

The CSV records one row per fresh process. Launch 0 is the first project load and first execution. Launches 1–3 each contain a startup/load observation and seven execution observations in time order. All times are milliseconds. [Per-process measurements](2026-11-10-seed42-samples.csv)
